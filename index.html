Orrery

An interactive 3D world. A marble rolls around the surface of a small planet where gravity always points at its centre, so you cannot fall off. Use the arrow keys or W, A, S and D to roll, or drag anywhere on a touch screen. Four unlit monuments stand on the surface, each naming a project. Rolling into one lights it and sounds a note; lighting all four breaks dawn over the whole planet. Progress is announced as each one lights.
