Orrery

An interactive 3D world. A marble rolls around the surface of a small planet where gravity always points at its centre, so you cannot fall off. Use the arrow keys or W, A, S and D to roll, space to jump, or drag anywhere on a touch screen. The view can be moved without moving the marble: Q and E turn it, R and F raise and lower it, or right-drag with a mouse, or drag with a second finger on a touch screen. Four unlit monuments stand on the surface, each naming a project, and each marked by a ring on the ground. Roll inside a ring to light that monument and sound a note; lighting all four breaks dawn over the whole planet. A lit monument's marker becomes a link to the project it names. Scattered between them are boulders, two rings of standing stones, three stone arches you can roll under, and clusters of crystals that ring when you strike them. A moon and a ringed companion planet hang in the sky and rise and set as you travel. The first visit is untimed; after that the run is clocked and your best time is kept, so a second visit is a race against it.
